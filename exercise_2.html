Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka is a beautiful island country located in the Indian Ocean. It is known for its rich culture, history, and natural beauty.

The capital city is Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte. The commercial capital is Colombo.

Facts about Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka has a population of about 22 million people. The country is famous for tea, spices, and tourism.

The highest mountain is Pidurutalagala. The longest river is Mahaweli.
