Department of Computer Science (DCS)

University of Jaffna



The Department of Computer Science (DCS) at the University of Jaffna is one of the leading academic departments in Sri Lanka. It offers high-quality education in computing, software engineering, and research.

The department focuses on both theoretical and practical knowledge. Students learn programming, algorithms, and systems design.

Programs Offered

BSc in Computer Science
BSc in Information Technology
MSc in Computer Science
